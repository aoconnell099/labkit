Three looks for the library

Each is the same library drawn a different way: a home with every piece live, by shelf, and a piece's own page. Workbench is the pick; the other two stay as they were, for comparison.

  1. B · Workbench picked

    Dense and practical. Grey chrome in light or dark round a themed canvas; an index down the side, paint cards for the themes, and an inspector beside the stage. A piece's page shows it in all themes at once, and a sample screen shows the pieces as one app page.
